Three steps. No repeated login routine.

    Save each account

  3. 03

    Switch from the toolbar

    Choose a saved account and Switchboard restores that session for you.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What to know before switching.

Can I use multiple accounts on the same website?

Yes. Save each signed-in account under a name, then choose the one you want from Switchboard. In Chrome, one saved account is active for that website at a time. In Firefox, containers can keep separate accounts open simultaneously.

Does Switchboard send my cookies anywhere?

Switchboard does not upload your saved cookies or account data to a developer-operated server. When you use a website, your browser still sends the active cookies to that website as part of normal browsing.

Will saved accounts stay signed in permanently?

Switchboard preserves the session cookies it can save, but websites remain in control of their sessions. A website can expire or revoke a login and may ask you to sign in again.

Can I move my saved accounts to another computer?

Yes. Create a password-encrypted .swb backup and restore it on another device using the same browser type: Chrome to Chrome or Firefox to Firefox.

Which websites does Switchboard support?

You choose which websites to add and grant access to. Compatibility depends on how each website manages authentication, cookies, and session expiry.
