HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No repeated login routine.

  1. 01

    Sign in normally

    Open the website and sign in to the first account as you usually would.

  2. 02

    Save each account

    Name the current login, then repeat once for any other account you need.

  3. 03

    Switch from the toolbar

    Choose a saved account and Switchboard restores that session for you.

PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE

Your sessions belong on your device.

Switchboard stores account labels, settings, and session data in your browser. The extension has no developer-operated server and does not upload your saved account data.

Read the privacy policy →
  • ✓
    No account required

    There is no Switchboard sign-up or cloud service.

  • ✓
    No analytics or advertising

    Your use of the extension is not tracked.

  • ✓
    You choose website access

    Permissions are requested for the websites you add.

BACKUP & RESTORE

Take your saved accounts with you.

Create a password-encrypted .swb backup and restore it on another device using the same browser type. You review its contents before anything is replaced.

⌁
Password encrypted

Switchboard cannot recover a forgotten backup password.
