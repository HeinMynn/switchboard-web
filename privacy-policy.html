SWITCHBOARD 1.0

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 9, 2026

Switchboard stores and processes your saved account data on your device. The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or automatic cloud sync.

Data on your device

Switchboard processes account labels, website domains, settings, and authentication cookies to provide the features you choose. Authentication cookies can include identifiers and tokens that grant access to an account.

Chrome

Saved cookie snapshots, account labels, website choices, the selected account, and onboarding status are stored in the extension's local browser storage. When you switch accounts, Switchboard reads open tab URLs to find tabs for the selected website. A temporary recovery record can contain affected tab IDs, original URLs, and cookie data; it is removed after the operation or recovery completes.

Firefox

Account labels, website choices, Firefox container IDs, and onboarding status are stored locally. Firefox manages each container's cookies and site storage. Switchboard reads cookies for a selected container when required for an export or other feature you request.

How data is used

Switchboard uses this data only to save, restore, switch, label, export, back up, or remove account entries, and to recover an interrupted operation. The developer does not receive your saved cookies or browsing activity from the extension.

Switchboard does not sell account data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility.

Backups and security

Backups are created only when you request them. Switchboard protects .swb backup contents with password-based encryption on your device before the file is downloaded. The backup can contain website settings, account labels, cookies, and the configuration needed to restore your accounts.

Your backup password is not sent to the developer and cannot be recovered by Switchboard. Anyone who obtains both the backup file and its password may be able to access the accounts represented by unexpired cookies.

Backups can be restored only to the same browser type: Chrome to Chrome or Firefox to Firefox. Firefox restoration creates replacement containers and remaps their identifiers. You can review an unlocked backup before confirming restoration. Restoration replaces the current Switchboard configuration and may keep a temporary recovery record if interrupted.

Your browser or operating system may save downloaded backups into a folder configured to sync with another service. That service's privacy terms then apply. Switchboard does not perform that upload.

Retention and deletion

Saved accounts and configuration remain until you remove them, uninstall the extension, or clear its browser storage. Recovery records remain only until recovery completes. Downloaded backup or export files remain wherever you save them until you delete them.

Remove Saved Account removes its saved Switchboard entry. Remove website removes the site's saved Switchboard entries. These actions do not necessarily delete live website cookies, sign you out, revoke website access, or delete Firefox containers. Use the website's logout and your browser's controls when you need those additional actions.

Permissions and your control

Chrome uses cookies, storage, tabs, downloads, and optional website access to manage selected website sessions, refresh affected tabs, and create files you request. Firefox uses storage, cookies, contextual identities, downloads, and optional website access for container-based account management and exports.

You choose which websites to add and when to create an export, backup, or restore. You can review and revoke website permissions from your browser's extension settings.

Websites, browsers, and other services

When you visit, sign in to, or refresh a website, your browser communicates with that website normally and sends the active cookies. The website's privacy policy applies to that communication. Switchboard cannot prevent a website from expiring or revoking a login session.

Your browser and extension store may separately process installation, update, and security information under their own policies. Switchboard's use of information received from Chrome APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and policy changes

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Switchboard Chrome Web Store listing or open an issue in the public source repository.

Material changes to Switchboard's data handling will be reflected in this policy and disclosed through the extension or its official listing where required.
